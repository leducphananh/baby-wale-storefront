## 2. S2.1 refinements locked

Recorded in `S2.1-…md` (top callout + targeted edits) and treated as approved here:

- **Sticky model (two-tier):** one compact persistent top navigation/header surface
  **AND** at most one bottom transactional/action sticky surface. Product mobile =
  sticky header + Add-to-Cart bottom bar. Cart mobile = sticky header + Checkout bottom
  bar. **Checkout mobile = sticky header only; the submit stays in-flow** (keyboard
  conflict). Never stack two bottom sticky bars; never filter + CTA both sticky; sticky
  UI must not eat excessive viewport height.
- **Order-success actions:** the success page *is* the token tracking view → no primary
  button that navigates to its own URL. Primary = **"Sao chép liên kết theo dõi"**
  (save/share); secondary = "Tiếp tục mua sắm". Status stays visible on the page.
- **Manual tracking input (`/tra-cuu-don-hang`):** accepts a raw token **or** a full
  Baby Wale tracking URL the customer pasted; the token is extracted from a valid Baby
  Wale URL before the lookup RPC. Backend lookup security unchanged; still **no
  `order_number`-only lookup**.
- **D1 LOCKED:** `completed` website order customer label = **"Đơn hàng đã được xác
  nhận"**. Never "Đã giao" or wording implying delivery.
- **D2 LOCKED:** no Add-to-Cart button on `ProductCard` in MVP — the card opens Product
  Detail.
- **D3 LOCKED:** Home uses a **static** hero/value block — no rotating carousel.
- **D4 OPEN:** no return/exchange policy is assumed or fabricated. The design leaves
  room for future trust/policy content without showing fake placeholders in production.

## 3. Brand / personality interpretation

The brief words — **Cute + Clean + Warm + Trustworthy + Modern e-commerce** — are a
feeling, not a design. Translated into working principles (before any color):

| Brief word | What it means *visually* for Baby Wale | What it must **not** become |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cute** | A few small, gentle brand moments (a whale/wave mark; a soft rounded corner; a friendly-but-legible type voice). Charm in the details. | A toy site. Cartoon scenes per section. Bouncy animation. Baby-talk type. |
| **Clean** | Generous whitespace; a strict, small component set; hairline structure over heavy chrome; one consistent rhythm everywhere. | Sterile/clinical. Enterprise-grey. Empty desktop canvases that collapse badly on mobile. |
| **Warm** | Soft neutral surfaces (not stark white glare), a warm accent used *sparingly*, human copy, real photos of real products. | Pink everywhere. Pastel-on-pastel mush. Saccharine. |
| **Trustworthy** | Confident price and availability; a calm, credible checkout; quiet factual trust cues; consistent, predictable layout. | Fake reviews/badges. Urgency banners. A checkout that looks like a promo landing page. |
| **Modern e-commerce** | Photography carries the page; a strong `ProductCard`; a fast, mobile-first, thumb-friendly conversion path; restrained motion. | Marketplace density and clutter. A giant gradient hero with a tiny "Shop" link. AI-landing-page tropes. |

**Perception goal (from S2.1 §5 / brief §5):** a customer should think *"a real,
trustworthy mother-and-baby shop; the product info is clear; I can safely order here; it
feels caring without looking childish."* Explicitly avoid reading as: toy site, generic
template, admin dashboard, dropshipping site, marketplace clone, AI landing page, luxury
fashion store, clinical/hospital site.

**Visual balance target (brief §7):** ~product photography + white/soft-neutral space +
quiet brand accents. The system **supports** products; it does not compete with them.

## 5. Reference observations

### 5.1 OBSERVED (competitor sites inspected this phase)

**None.** No live competitor or reference storefront was inspected. S2.3 may do so with
rights/access; if it does, its notes must be labelled OBSERVED and dated.

### 5.2 GENERAL DESIGN PRINCIPLES applied (from UX literature — §30)

- **Mobile-first is non-negotiable:** most retail e-commerce traffic and orders are on
  phones; design the 390px layout first, scale up. *(ecorn, Shopify, DBB.)*
- **Product page essentials:** high-quality images with multiple angles/zoom; **3+
  thumbnails visible even in list context**; clear, specific titles and descriptions
  (materials, size, weight, care); all options/variants immediately visible; breadcrumbs
  matter *more* on mobile because users lose context; ≤3 taps to any product. *(Baymard,
  VWO, Toptal.)*
- **Product card:** the **price must be prominent** (larger, bold, visually separated)
  and use **one consistent format across every view**; square cards work well on mobile;
  start 1-column mobile → 2 tablet → 3–4 desktop; typography must stay legible at small
  sizes. *(FoxEcom, UX Collective, design-bootcamp.)*
- **Checkout:** guest checkout is essential (forced registration is a top abandonment
  cause); **one-page checkout reduces abandonment** by removing progress anxiety and
  back-button drop-off; **trust signals placed adjacent to the sensitive fields** reduce
  the anxiety spike. *(ecorn, Salesforce, CartBoss.)*

### 5.3 MARKET DATA POINTS (Vietnam, 2025 — §30)

- **~57%** of Vietnam online purchases are made on mobile; **~92%** of shoppers use
  smartphones; **50%** shop online weekly. *(TGM Research.)*
- **COD is the most preferred payment method (~35%)**; bank transfer is a solid
  secondary method. *(TGM Research; 2C2P.)* → validates S0's COD + manual bank transfer
  MVP and the need for a *clear, trusted* payment-instructions surface on the success
  page.

## 6. Three visual directions

All three share the **identical S2.1 IA, navigation, flows, and component set**. They
differ only in visual personality. They are genuinely different design languages — not a
"blue vs pink vs purple" recolor.

---

### Direction A — **Soft Trust Commerce**

- **Emotional impression:** a calm, competent, caring shop. "This is a real store and my
  order is safe here." Warmth comes from softness and photography, not saturation.
- **Whitespace / density:** airy-but-efficient. Generous gutters and vertical rhythm;
  intentional middle-ground catalog density (2-up mobile with readable name + price +
  a good image).
- **Color character:** light neutral canvas; **blue as the structural trust anchor**
  (header accents, links, the primary CTA); **pink/coral as a small warmth accent**
  (cart badge, a few highlights, illustration moments) — never a large fill. Near-zero
  decorative gradients.
- **Typography character:** humanist, friendly-but-credible; strong, clear numerals for
  price; confident (not oversized) headings.
- **Shape / radius character:** gentle, consistent rounding — soft rectangles for cards,
  fields, buttons; **not** pills, **not** blobs.
- **Imagery treatment:** real product photos on a consistent light frame, `contain`-fit
  with padding so labels are never cropped; one aspect ratio site-wide.
- **Icon / illustration:** one simple line-icon set; a handful of small whale/wave brand
  moments (hero, empty states, missing-image placeholder). Photography stays primary.
- **CTA character:** one clear blue primary button per context, sentence case, calm — no
  caps, no pulsing, no urgency.
- **ProductCard character:** image-dominant, quiet metadata, **price is the loudest
  text after the image**; stock badge only visually loud when "Hết hàng".
- **Trust-block character:** small icon + short factual line, grouped, low-key; present
  on Home / PDP / Checkout / Success / Footer.
- **Mobile feeling:** roomy, thumb-friendly, predictable; one compact sticky header +
  one bottom action bar where allowed.
- **Strengths:** best balance of warmth + credibility; photography-forward; ages well;
  easiest to keep consistent; strongest for checkout trust; directly satisfies the brief
  words and the S2.1 north-star tests.
- **Risks:** if executed timidly it can read a little "safe/expected"; the brand
  personality must be earned in the small details (mark, micro-copy, one or two motifs)
  or it drifts toward generic.
- **Fit for Baby Wale:** **high.** Matches the whale/blue/pink identity, the mother-baby
  warmth, Vietnamese retail expectations, and a mobile-first guest-checkout MVP.

---

### Direction B — **Playful Modern Family**

- **Emotional impression:** upbeat, friendly, family-forward. More visible "cute":
  rounded everything, a warmer/brighter palette, small illustrations as recurring
  characters.
- **Whitespace / density:** medium; more colored surfaces and tinted section bands break
  the page into "zones".
- **Color character:** warmer and more present — pink/coral used at *section* scale
  (tinted bands, colored category tiles), blue as a secondary. More willingness to tint
  surfaces.
- **Typography character:** rounder, softer letterforms; slightly larger, more
  expressive headings; friendlier than functional.
- **Shape / radius character:** large radii; pill buttons; rounded "bubble" containers.
- **Imagery treatment:** photos often on tinted or shaped (rounded/organic) backgrounds;
  more lifestyle framing where available.
- **Icon / illustration:** a richer illustrated layer — a whale mascot recurring across
  sections, decorative motifs (bubbles, waves) more prominent.
- **CTA character:** bold, rounded, colorful; more visual "pop".
- **ProductCard character:** softer, more decorated frame; brand personality on the card
  itself; risk of the frame competing with the product.
- **Trust-block character:** friendlier, more illustrated; risk of feeling marketing-y
  rather than factual.
- **Mobile feeling:** lively, colorful; more visual energy per screen.
- **Strengths:** most distinctive and memorable; leans hardest into "cute + warm";
  appealing to a young-parent audience; a clear brand character.
- **Risks:** **highest risk of "toy site" / "childish"**; colored bands and large radii
  fight product photography and price legibility; **checkout can read as unserious**
  (fails the S2.1 trust test); pastel/tint layering threatens AA contrast;
  illustration-per-section is a **maintenance and consistency burden**; ages faster.
- **Fit for Baby Wale:** **medium-low.** The personality is on-brand but the execution
  risks undermine trust, product clarity, and checkout credibility — the things S2.1
  ranked highest.

---

### Direction C — **Premium Calm Baby**

- **Emotional impression:** quiet, editorial, "considered". Restraint and refinement;
  fewer, larger elements; lots of air.
- **Whitespace / density:** very airy. Large hero whitespace, low catalog density,
  generous type spacing.
- **Color character:** near-monochrome neutral canvas; blue and pink reduced to *very*
  subtle accents (thin rules, small marks); color is almost withheld.
- **Typography character:** more contrast between a refined display heading and a quiet
  body; typography does the expressive work.
- **Shape / radius character:** minimal rounding; crisp edges; hairline rules; a
  "gallery" feel.
- **Imagery treatment:** photography treated as hero art — large, generous margins,
  consistent light styling assumed.
- **Icon / illustration:** almost none; a single restrained brand mark.
- **CTA character:** understated — often a bordered/ghost button or a quiet solid; relies
  on placement and space, not color, for prominence.
- **ProductCard character:** minimal, lots of padding, few products per screen; elegant
  but low information density.
- **Trust-block character:** extremely quiet — thin rules and small text.
- **Mobile feeling:** spacious and clean, but **fewer products above the fold** and a
  softer CTA presence.
- **Strengths:** looks the most "designed" and premium; excellent typographic hierarchy;
  very low visual-noise; strong if product photography is uniformly high quality.
- **Risks:** **assumes consistent, high-end product photography Baby Wale will not have**
  (supplier photos vary); **low density wastes scarce mobile space** and hurts
  browse-ability for a catalog with many similar SKUs (formula stages, diaper sizes);
  understated CTAs can **weaken conversion** and the primary-action clarity S2.1
  requires; can read as **cold / fashion-store**, not caring mother-baby; "cute" is
  nearly absent.
- **Fit for Baby Wale:** **medium-low.** Beautiful in the abstract, but it fights the
  real content (mixed-source photos, dense similar SKUs) and the commercial goals.

## 8. Recommended direction

**Recommend Direction A — Soft Trust Commerce** as the input to S2.3.

It was not chosen mechanically. Against the three explicit S2.1 tests:

- **North-star = Product Detail at ~390px (S2.1 §47).** A makes product image → name →
  price → availability → quantity → "Thêm vào giỏ" → trust read as one calm, confident
  block. B's colored bands and large radii compete with the photo and price; C's
  understated CTA and low density weaken the purchase moment. **A passes clearly.**
- **ProductCard reusability (S2.1 §48)** across catalog / category / search / related /
  home. A's image-dominant, quiet-metadata, loud-price card works at 2-up/390px and
  scales to 3–5 up on desktop without redecorating. B's decorated frame competes with
  the product and is heavier to keep consistent; C's low density is wasteful for a dense
  catalog of similar SKUs. **A wins.**
- **Checkout trust (S2.1 §49).** A's calm, credible checkout supports name/phone/address
  + COD/bank-transfer with quiet trust cues adjacent to the payment section (matching
  published checkout guidance). B risks an "unserious" checkout. **A wins.**

A also best balances: mother-baby warmth (soft surfaces, photography, a light whale
motif) with trust (confident price, calm checkout, quiet factual trust blocks); product
clarity; Vietnamese mobile-first, COD-heavy retail expectations; longevity (restraint
ages well); and **ease of consistent implementation** — the single biggest risk for an
AI-built multi-page storefront (`visual-consistency`).

**Distinctiveness is A's one soft spot.** It is mitigated deliberately, not by adding
noise: (1) a small, consistent whale/wave brand mark used in a few defined places; (2) a
warm — not stark — neutral canvas; (3) the pink accent placed with intent (cart badge,
"mới"/highlight chips, one or two illustration moments); (4) a friendly type voice; (5)
distinctive, generous product framing. These are refined in S2.3/S2.4.

**This is a recommendation, not the approved design.** S2.4 remains the approval gate.

## 9. Selected visual doctrine

*(Format per brief §45. This becomes S2.3's design brief.)*

**Baby Wale should feel:** like a real, caring mother-and-baby shop that is also
visibly competent to take an order — calm, clean, warm, quietly friendly, trustworthy.

**The page should prioritize:** the product (photo, name, price, availability) and the
one primary action, in that order — before any decoration, banner, or brand storytelling.

**Surfaces should feel:** light and soft — a warm near-white canvas, hairline structure,
generous whitespace. Structure comes from space, tone, and thin borders before shadows.

**Brand accents should:** guide attention, not decorate everything. Blue is the
structural trust anchor (primary action, links, key accents). Pink/coral is a small
warmth accent (cart badge, highlight chips, a few illustration moments) — never a large
background fill, never on most surfaces. Near-zero decorative gradients.

**Product photography should:** carry the commercial content. Every product image sits
on a consistent light frame at one shared aspect ratio, `contain`-fit with padding so
packaging text is never cropped, normalizing inconsistent supplier sources. A branded
neutral placeholder covers missing images — never a broken-image icon or a stock photo.

**Typography should:** be humanist, highly legible at small mobile sizes, excellent for
Vietnamese diacritics, with strong clear numerals for price. Headings are confident, not
billboard-sized. One family, multiple weights (keep **Be Vietnam Pro** — §11).

**CTAs should:** be one clear blue primary action per context, sentence case, calm — no
all-caps, no pulsing, no countdowns, no urgency language. Secondary actions are visibly
quieter (ghost/link).

**Trust information should:** be quiet and factual — a small icon plus a short true
statement, grouped, repeated consistently on Home / Product Detail / Checkout / Success /
Footer. No fake reviews, counts, certifications, guarantees, return windows, or delivery
promises. Genuine-product claims only with explicit owner-approved wording.

**Mobile should:** be the primary canvas (~390px). 2-column product grid, thumb-reachable
primary actions, one compact sticky header + at most one bottom action bar (never on
checkout), bottom sheets for filter/sort, a collapsible checkout summary, no horizontal
scroll except deliberate carousels, touch targets comfortably ≥ ~44px.

**Never:** pink-everywhere or blue-everywhere backgrounds; pastel-on-pastel low contrast;
a giant gradient hero with a tiny commerce entry; a rotating carousel as the hero; pills
and blobs as the default shape; a drop shadow on every card; different radius/shadow/type
per page; random decorative blobs / arbitrary gradient circles; more than one primary CTA
color; marketplace-tiny type; huge desktop whitespace that collapses badly on mobile;
hover-only behavior; emoji as the production icon system; product images cropped through
packaging text; a checkout styled like a marketing page; fabricated social proof; a
visual language that changes page to page.

## 10. Color direction

**Roles only. No final hex, RGB, or Tailwind values — S2.4 owns the palette.** The only
literal reference is that the existing Baby Wale **logo already pairs a blue with a
pink**; S2.4 will sample the exact anchor values from the supplied brand asset.

| Role | Family / character | Usage intent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Trust anchor** | Blue family — a *friendly mid-tone* (confident, not navy-corporate, not baby-powder pale) | Primary button, links, active states, key structural accents, focus ring base |
| **Warm accent** | Pink / coral / warm-rose family | Cart count badge, "mới"/highlight chips, small illustration moments, selected-state tint — **small areas only** |
| **Primary surface** | White / warm near-white | Page background, cards, sheets |
| **Secondary surface** | A very light neutral — *explore both* a cool blue-grey and a warm off-white | Section separation, input fills, summary panels |
| **Text (primary)** | Deep neutral / near-navy (not pure black) | Body, headings |
| **Text (muted)** | Mid neutral with **AA contrast on the surfaces it's used on** | Metadata, helper text, breadcrumbs |
| **Border / hairline** | Low-contrast neutral | Dividers, card edges, header underline |
| **Success / in-stock** | Natural green family | "Còn hàng" badge, "Đơn hàng đã được xác nhận" chip, success mark |
| **Warning** | Warm amber family | Cautionary notices (e.g. rate-limited) |
| **Danger / out-of-stock** | Red family (accessible; **always paired with icon + text**) | "Hết hàng", blocking cart states, field errors |
| **Focus ring** | High-contrast, visible on every surface (likely blue-based, possibly a paired outer/inner) | Keyboard focus — never removed |

**Restraint rules (brief §14):** no pink background across pages; no blue background
across pages; no gradient on every section; the **primary CTA color never changes by
page**; **no rainbow category cards** (one surface tone + sparing accent, not a different
hue per category).

**EXPLORATORY — NOT APPROVED TOKEN:** for S2.3 mockups only, a working stand-in family
might be *a sky-to-cornflower blue*, *a soft warm rose*, and *a warm paper neutral*.
These are placeholders to make screens legible, not the palette.

## 11. Typography direction

**Do not change the production font, do not install another font (brief §15, §54).** S1
ships **Be Vietnam Pro** via `next/font` (`latin` + `vietnamese` subsets, weights
400/500/600/700).

**Evaluation for Direction A:** Be Vietnam Pro is a humanist sans purpose-built for
Vietnamese, with complete, well-designed diacritics, several weights, clear lining
numerals, and good legibility at small sizes. Its character is *friendly but credible* —
neither clinical nor childish. It suits Soft Trust Commerce across the full range
(warm hero heading → dense product cards → a serious checkout).

**Recommendation: keep Be Vietnam Pro** as the single primary family for S2.4. One
family, multiple weights — **do not add a second/display font for MVP** (extra payload,
extra consistency surface, no clear benefit). If S2.4 wants a touch more hero
personality, the lever is weight/size/spacing within Be Vietnam Pro, not a new face.
*(If a genuinely stronger option surfaces, document it for S2.4 review — do not switch
now.)*

**Hierarchy character (qualitative — no px/rem; S2.4 sets the scale):**

| Role | Character |
| --- | --- |
| Hero / H1 | Confident, medium-large, semibold — a clear page voice, **not** billboard marketing type |
| Section heading (H2/H3) | Clearly subordinate to H1; calm; consistent step down |
| Product name | High readability first; regular/medium weight; 2-line clamp with reserved height |
| **Price** | The strongest text anchor after the image — larger + semibold/bold, lining/tabular numerals, one format everywhere via `Price` |
| Body | Comfortable, compact retail reading; relaxed line-height; never marketplace-tiny |
| Metadata (brand, unit, breadcrumb, helper) | Quiet — muted color, smaller, low emphasis |
| CTA label | Clear, medium weight, sentence case — not all-caps, not shouty |
| Status chip / badge | Small, legible, always text (+ icon), never size-only or color-only |

**Vietnamese specifics:** verify diacritic clearance at the smallest card sizes (stacked
marks like ế, ữ, ậ must not clip against tight line-heights); prefer slightly looser
line-height on dense cards for this reason.

## 12. Shape & elevation direction

**Shape (no final radius tokens — S2.4):**

- **Gentle, consistent rounding.** Soft rectangles for `ProductCard`, category tiles,
  fields, buttons, badges, sheets/drawers, summary panels — a small-to-medium radius,
  the *same* radius family across all pages.
- **Not** full pills for buttons (a lightly rounded rectangle reads more "commerce /
  trust" than a pill). **Not** giant blob/organic radii.
- Product images: a small radius on card/thumbnail frames; the PDP hero image can use a
  minimal-to-no radius so the photo breathes — decide the exact pairing in S2.4, but
  keep it to **one radius scale**, applied consistently, not per-page.
- Where restraint is better than softness: data-dense areas (specs list, order summary
  rows), hairline dividers, and the checkout form — crisp, quiet, not bubbly.

**Elevation (no final shadow tokens — S2.4):** structure first through **whitespace →
surface tone → hairline border → hierarchy**; shadow is the last resort.

| Level | Where | Character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flat | Most content, static `ProductCard`, sections | No shadow; separated by space / hairline / tone |
| Subtle raise | Hovered/focused interactive card, small popovers | One very light, tight shadow — barely there |
| Overlay | Cart drawer, bottom sheet, modal, sticky header (when scrolled), sticky bottom action bar | A soft, single overlay shadow **plus a top hairline** to detach it from content — restrained, not a heavy drop shadow |

**Never:** a drop shadow on every card; multiple shadow "languages"; different elevation
per page.

## 13. Photography / image direction

Product photography is **the** commercial content and the biggest consistency risk
(supplier photos vary wildly). The visual system's job is to normalize them without
touching the source files.

| Surface | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| **ProductCard image** | One shared **aspect ratio site-wide** — recommend **square (1:1)** (safest for mixed packaging shapes and 2-up mobile). A consistent light frame (white / warm near-white). `object-fit: contain` with internal padding so the product floats with breathing room and **label text is never cropped**. `next/image` with correct `sizes` for the card slot. |
| **Product Detail hero** | Same light frame; square or 4:5 (pick one in S2.4 and keep it). Large, generous margins, swipeable on mobile with a dot/thumbnail indicator. `contain`-fit, padded. This is the LCP image → `priority`; everything else lazy. |
| **Gallery thumbnails** | Same frame + ratio, small; ≥3 visible where they exist (matches published PDP guidance); active thumbnail clearly indicated (border + `aria-current`), keyboard-navigable. |
| **Missing-image state** | A **branded neutral placeholder**: soft surface + a small whale/box line motif + the product name. **Never** a broken-image glyph, **never** a stock/lifestyle substitute. |
| **Related products** | Identical `ProductCard` image treatment — no special-case. |
| **Home "Sản phẩm mới"** | Identical `ProductCard`. |
| **Category tiles** | See §26-D / §27 — icon-led by default, photo-thumbnail only if a consistent set exists. |

**Rules:** do not alter, recolor, or re-background source product images; the *frame +
consistent background + `contain` + padding + fixed ratio* do the normalizing. Reserve
the image box before load (no layout shift). Public, crawlable URLs from the future
storefront public bucket (O3); never private admin signed URLs (`next-image-storefront`).

## 14. ProductCard direction

The single most reused surface (catalog, category, search, related, home). **One
definition** (`visual-consistency`). Content order is fixed by S2.1 §5.3: image → name →
brand → price → unit → availability → (open-detail affordance). **No Add-to-Cart button
(D2).**

- **Image dominance:** the photo is the largest element — square, on the consistent
  light frame, product centered with breathing room.
- **Information grouping:** a compact text block under the image. Brand as a *quiet*
  small label (above the name, or muted right after it — pick one, keep it). Name in
  2 lines max with **reserved height** so price/availability never get pushed out of
  view by a long name.
- **Price emphasis:** the loudest text on the card — the `Price` treatment (§11),
  consistent format, integer VND. It should be findable in well under a second (matches
  published card guidance).
- **Unit:** shown only when it changes meaning ("Lon 800g", "Bịch 64 miếng") — quiet,
  near the name or price.
- **Availability:** `StockBadge` (text + icon, never color-only). Visually calm for
  "Còn hàng" (or omitted when in stock, if S2.4 prefers); clearly visible for
  **"Hết hàng"**.
- **Hover / focus (desktop):** subtle — a gentle lift or border emphasis, optional tiny
  image scale (respect `prefers-reduced-motion`). A **visible `:focus-visible` ring
  always** — the whole card is one link/tap target to detail.
- **Mobile 2-column:** must remain readable at ~390px — name legible, price prominent,
  image large enough to judge the product. 1-column only if content truly forces it.
- **Long-name handling:** 2-line clamp + ellipsis; layout below the name is unaffected.
- **Out-of-stock character:** the card stays **full-color and legible** (not greyed into
  near-invisibility). A clear "Hết hàng" badge; at most a very light image de-emphasis
  that still meets contrast. Fully tappable to detail.
- **Density:** intentional middle ground — comfortable, scannable, not marketplace-dense,
  not one-per-screen airy.

**Stress-test names** to design against (mock; §51): "Sữa bột Meiji Infant Formula số 0
– lon 800g", "Bỉm/tã dán Merries size XL 44 miếng (12–20kg)", "Bình sữa Comotomo cổ rộng
250ml chống sặc", "Khăn ướt Huggies không mùi gói 80 tờ (combo 3)". The card must look
right with these, not only with "Sữa Meiji".

## 15. Product Detail direction

**The north-star screen.** A visual direction that cannot make this excellent at ~390px
is rejected. Content order fixed by S2.1 §6 / §14.1.

**Mobile (top to bottom):**

1. Compact breadcrumb (`‹ [Danh mục]`).
2. **Gallery** — large square image on the consistent frame; swipeable; dot/thumbnail
   indicator; tap to enlarge (dialog). LCP → `priority`.
3. **Purchase block** — calm, generously spaced:
   - Product **name** (H1, confident, strong).
   - **Brand** (quiet).
   - **Price** — the largest price treatment on the site.
   - **`StockBadge`** — "Còn hàng" / "Hết hàng" (text + icon).
   - **Unit** (quiet, e.g. "Lon 800g").
   - **Quantity stepper** — comfortably sized +/− (≥ ~44px), value field, labelled.
   - **Primary CTA** — one blue "Thêm vào giỏ" button, full-width, calm. **Not**
     aggressive: no caps, no pulse, no urgency/countdown, no "MUA NGAY".
4. **Sticky bottom Add-to-Cart bar** — the *one* bottom transactional surface; appears
   after the purchase block scrolls away. Compact: mini price + "Thêm vào giỏ"
   (quantity optional). Detached from content by a top hairline + soft overlay shadow;
   limited height.
5. **Description** — readable measure; real content, not lorem.
6. **Specs** — a quiet key/value list: xuất xứ / nhà sản xuất / nhà phân phối. **Never**
   supplier, cost, batch, expiry, or internal stock numbers.
7. **Trust block** — quiet (delivery & payment note, human-confirmation line, store
   contact).
8. **Related products** — the same `ProductCard`.

**Out-of-stock variant:** price still shown; `StockBadge` = **"Hết hàng"**; the primary
button renders **disabled with the label "Hết hàng"** (still clearly a button, clearly
inactive); quantity disabled; the sticky bar mirrors the disabled state; **related
products given more presence** so there's a path forward. The full description/specs stay
readable.

**Desktop:** gallery left, purchase panel right (the panel may stick within the viewport
as the page scrolls); description + specs + related full-width below. Semantic DOM order
unchanged (gallery → purchase panel → description → specs → related).

**Feel target:** the purchase action is **obvious, safe, easy, not aggressive.**

## 16. Header / search direction

IA is fixed by S2.1 §3.1–§3.2 (header + hamburger; search & cart in the header; no
login). Visual character:

- **Visual weight:** light. A mostly-white header with a **hairline bottom border** — no
  heavy shadow at rest. When stuck/scrolled it may gain a faint hairline/very light
  shadow to detach from content. Compact height (it is always sticky — it must not eat
  the viewport).
- **Logo scale:** modest — present, recognizable, not dominant. Left on desktop, centered
  on mobile.
- **Search prominence:** **high — second only to the logo.** Desktop: a visible input
  occupying real width (center zone). Mobile: a **full-width field in its own row** under
  the top bar, always visible (per S2.1). Search reads as a primary tool, not an
  afterthought icon.
- **Nav density:** minimal — "Sản phẩm" + a "Danh mục" affordance; low visual noise;
  "Tra cứu đơn hàng" / "Liên hệ" are quiet secondary links (or in the hamburger on
  mobile).
- **Cart:** icon + a small count badge in the **pink accent** (or a strong neutral) —
  legible, not alarming; `aria-label` includes the count.
- **Border / elevation behavior:** hairline at rest → faint elevation when stuck. Never a
  thick colored bar.
- **Mobile top bar:** hamburger (left) · logo (center) · cart+count (right); search row
  beneath.

## 17. Cart direction

Cart is about **confidence, not excitement** (S2.1 §8, §26).

- **Item separation:** clear — hairline dividers or gentle card grouping per line. Each
  line: image (card frame treatment, smaller), name, unit, quantity stepper, line total,
  remove.
- **Quantity controls:** comfortably sized +/− (≥ ~44px), labelled ("Tăng số lượng" /
  "Giảm số lượng"); value visible.
- **Remove affordance:** a small icon button with an accessible name that includes the
  product ("Xoá [tên] khỏi giỏ") — findable, not hidden behind a swipe-only gesture.
- **Totals hierarchy:** **"Tổng tiền hàng"** is the strong figure. **"Phí vận chuyển:
  Nhân viên sẽ xác nhận"** is plain informative text — **not** an error/warning style.
  "Tạm tính (hàng hoá)" above it, quiet.
- **Price-change state:** an **informational** treatment — a softly highlighted row + a
  short line ("Giá đã thay đổi") + old price struck-through and muted, new price in the
  normal `Price` style; the subtotal updates with a small "đã cập nhật" note. The primary
  CTA relabels to "Xác nhận giá mới & tiếp tục". **The page must not look like an error
  screen.**
- **Stock-change / unavailable / out-of-stock states:** a calm but clearly **blocking**
  treatment on the affected line (message + inline action: "Cập nhật số lượng còn N" /
  "Xoá"); the sticky CTA is disabled with a short reason. Blocking ≠ alarming — one
  accent, not a red wash.
- **Empty cart:** friendly one-liner + "Xem sản phẩm" + category shortcuts. Optional
  small whale/wave motif. Never a dead end.
- **Mobile:** sticky bottom bar — "Tổng tiền hàng" + "Tiến hành đặt hàng" (the one bottom
  transactional surface). Detached by a top hairline + soft shadow.
- **Drawer vs page:** the drawer is a compact projection of the same visual language —
  same line treatment, same totals, same `Price`/`StockBadge` — just condensed.

## 18. Checkout direction

Checkout should feel **calm, short, safe, focused** (S2.1 §9, §27). Published guidance:
single-page reduces abandonment; trust signals belong next to the sensitive fields;
guest checkout is expected.

- **Chrome:** minimal — logo + a quiet "Quay lại giỏ hàng" link; **no** marketing
  banners, cross-sell, promo strips, or full nav; a slim footer at most.
- **Section grouping:** clearly separated, calm groups: **Thông tin nhận hàng** →
  **Phương thức thanh toán** → **Xem lại đơn hàng** → submit. Section headings quiet,
  consistent.
- **Field rhythm:** one field per row on mobile; **visible labels above fields**
  (placeholder is never the label); generous vertical spacing; a clear
  `:focus-visible` state; correct input types (`tel` / `inputmode` for phone).
- **Errors:** inline, directly under the field, in a **calm** treatment (icon + short
  message, associated via `aria-describedby` + `aria-invalid`) — not a shouting red
  block. A submit-time error summary moves focus to the first invalid field.
- **Payment options:** a bordered selectable **row per method** (radio control + label +
  one helper line: "Thanh toán khi nhận hàng (COD)" / "Chuyển khoản ngân hàng"). The
  selected row is indicated by **border + check + a subtle tint** — not color alone.
- **Order summary:** quiet, structured. **Mobile:** collapsed at top (shows item count +
  "Tổng tiền hàng"), expandable; full summary also directly above the submit. **Desktop:**
  a **sticky right aside**. "Phí vận chuyển: Nhân viên sẽ xác nhận" as plain text; the
  sum labelled "Tổng tiền hàng", never "Tổng thanh toán".
- **Reassurance / trust:** one calm line near the submit ("Nhân viên sẽ liên hệ xác nhận
  đơn hàng.") plus the quiet trust cues (COD/transfer accepted, human confirmation)
  placed **adjacent to the payment section / submit** — restrained, no badges or
  certifications we don't have.
- **Pending:** submit shows "Đang xử lý…" + spinner + disabled; the form dims slightly /
  becomes read-only. **No full-screen takeover.**
- **Blocked (price/stock changed):** a calm inline notice above the submit; the submit is
  disabled/relabelled until re-confirmed; **form values and cart preserved**.
- **Submit placement:** **in-flow at the end of the form on mobile** (no bottom sticky
  transactional bar — refinement A). Full-width, clearly the one primary action.

## 19. Success / tracking direction

The success page must say **"the store received your order"** — not "shipment complete"
(S2.1 §11, §20.5, §28).

- **Tone:** a calm, positive confirmation — a **modest** success mark + heading. **No**
  large celebratory full-screen animation, confetti, or "Hoàn tất!" implying delivery.
- **Hierarchy:**
  1. **Confirmation heading** — "Đặt hàng thành công" (received & submitted).
  2. **Order code** (`order_number`) — prominent, **selectable**, with a copy affordance.
  3. **Status chip** — "Đơn mới – chờ xác nhận" as a `StockBadge`-style chip (icon +
     text, info tone) + one line "Nhân viên sẽ liên hệ với bạn để xác nhận đơn hàng."
  4. **Payment instructions** (method-specific):
     - **COD** — a short calm note.
     - **Bank transfer** — a **visually prominent, clearly structured block**: bank /
       account name / account number / branch *(placeholder until O8)*, **memo = the
       order code**, each with a copy affordance; "Đơn hàng được xử lý sau khi cửa hàng
       xác nhận đã nhận chuyển khoản." **Payment is not marked complete.**
  5. **Order summary** — quiet, read-only, safe fields only.
  6. **Tracking-link utility** — primary action **"Sao chép liên kết theo dõi"** + a
     "Lưu lại đường dẫn này" hint. **No "Theo dõi đơn hàng" button that navigates to this
     same URL.**
  7. **Support contact** — phone / Zalo.
  8. **"Tiếp tục mua sắm"** — the secondary action.
- **Tracking page (`/tra-cuu-don-hang`):** one calm field — "Nhập mã theo dõi hoặc dán
  liên kết theo dõi đơn hàng" — one "Tra cứu" submit. The **found** view reuses the exact
  success layout. **Not found / invalid** = one calm, non-revealing message
  ("Không tìm thấy đơn hàng với mã này…"). Loading = inline. No raw errors.

## 20. Trust-system direction

A single, quiet, repeatable pattern used consistently on **Home, Product Detail,
Checkout, Success, Footer** (S2.1 §13, brief §30):

- **Form:** a small line icon + a short, **true** statement. Grouped 2–4 items. Low
  emphasis — muted text, no colored panels, no big badges. It should feel *dependable*,
  not like fine print and not like marketing.
- **Allowed factual concepts:** real store contact (phone / address / Zalo — when
  provided); "Thanh toán khi nhận hàng (COD) hoặc chuyển khoản"; **every order is
  confirmed by a staff member before processing**; genuine-product / clear-origin
  wording **only with explicit owner-approved exact text** (see §29 VD4).
- **Forbidden (do not design slots that imply these unless real content exists):** star
  ratings, review counts, "X người đã mua", trust/security certification badges,
  money-back guarantees, specific delivery-time promises, invented return windows,
  "100% chính hãng" **unless the owner explicitly approves that claim**.
- **D4:** the footer/PDP may reserve *structure* for a future "Chính sách" block, but
  **nothing renders in production until real policy content is supplied** — no lorem, no
  placeholder promises.

## 21. Icon / illustration direction

- **Iconography:** **one** consistent set — simple, modern **line icons**, a single
  stroke weight (an optional restrained duotone/accent for a few brand moments only).
  Icons **clarify, never decorate every line**. For unfamiliar commerce concepts, icon +
  text always (never icon-only). **No emoji** as the production icon system. No mixed
  outline/filled/third-party families. The specific icon library is chosen at
  implementation (a single lightweight set) — **not installed now** (brief §54).
- **Illustration:** **minimal.** A few small, defined brand moments — a simple
  whale/wave mark — in the hero, empty states, and the missing-image placeholder.
  **No** large cartoon scenes per section, **no** recurring mascot across every band.
  Product photography stays primary.
- **Decorative motifs:** at most a subtle wave/curve edge or a faint bubble motif in one
  or two brand moments (hero edge, footer, empty states), very low contrast, optional
  (zero is acceptable). **Never** generic blobs or arbitrary gradient circles — those
  read as AI-landing-page filler.

## 22. Motion direction

Qualitative rules (no animation library — brief §34, §54):

- **Good / allowed:** short functional transitions (~150–250ms feel) — cart drawer
  slide-in, bottom sheet open/close, add-to-cart toast, checkout-summary expand/collapse,
  subtle hover/focus feedback, image cross-fade in the gallery.
- **Avoid:** autoplay hero, parallax, scroll-triggered entrance animations, constantly
  floating decorations, anything where **motion is required to understand state**,
  celebratory success animations.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion`:** reduce or remove all non-essential motion; never gate
  information or feedback behind an animation. (S1 already ships a global
  `prefers-reduced-motion` CSS block — keep and extend that discipline.)

## 23. Mobile behavior

~390px is the primary canvas (VN market: ~57% of purchases on mobile).

- **Product grid:** **2 columns** is the recommended default at ~390px — with a legible
  name, a prominent price, and an image large enough to judge the product. 1-column only
  if specific content truly requires it. **Desktop:** 3–5 cards by container width — not
  marketplace-dense.
- **Sticky model:** compact sticky header + **one** bottom action bar where allowed
  (Product: Add-to-Cart; Cart: Checkout). **Checkout: none** — submit in-flow.
- **Touch targets:** comfortably ≥ ~44px, with spacing so adjacent targets (steppers,
  remove, filter chips, pagination) aren't mis-tapped.
- **Filter / sort:** bottom sheets (focus-managed), not a cramped inline control row.
- **Checkout summary:** collapsible at top + repeated above the submit.
- **No horizontal page scroll** at 390px — except deliberate carousels (gallery,
  "Sản phẩm mới" strip, related products). Long names/prices wrap or clamp.
- **Density:** intentional middle ground — enough products above the fold to browse a
  catalog of similar SKUs, without marketplace clutter.

## 24. Accessibility visual constraints

Moodboard choices must **support** (not fight) the `accessibility` skill. S2.3/S2.4 must
hold to:

- **AA contrast** for body text, prices, badges, helper text, and the focus ring against
  every surface they appear on. (S2.4 verifies with real values.)
- **A visible `:focus-visible` style on every interactive element** — never `outline:
  none` without a stronger replacement.
- **Status is never color-only** — `StockBadge`, price-changed / out-of-stock notices,
  order-status chips, selected payment method: all pair color with text and/or icon and
  shape.
- **Readable body text** — no marketplace-tiny sizes; comfortable line-height; diacritic
  clearance at the smallest card sizes.
- **Touch targets** comfortably large; **labels visible** (not placeholder-only);
  **errors visible** and associated with their field.
- **Reduced motion** honored; **nothing hover-only** (tap/keyboard equivalents for
  gallery zoom, menus, tooltips).

**Reject any direction that depends on:** very pale text; color-only state; tiny
controls; hover-only discovery; low-contrast pastel-on-pastel. *(This is a concrete
reason Direction B's tinted-band, large-pastel treatment is risky and Direction C's very
muted accents / understated CTAs are risky.)*

**Dark mode:** **not for MVP.** A customer retail storefront launches light-first;
dark-mode would roughly double the visual scope with no business value now. The S1
`@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` block in `globals.css` is a **create-next-app
scaffold leftover, not an approved product requirement** — S2.4/S3 should remove it or
deliberately re-scope it as part of establishing the real light palette. *(No code
change in S2.2.)*

## 25. Anti-pattern list

For S2.3 / S2.4 review. Reject:

1. Pink everywhere (pink as a large-surface background across pages).
2. Blue everywhere (blue as a large-surface background across pages).
3. Pastel-on-pastel low contrast; pale text that fails AA.
4. A giant gradient hero with a tiny commerce entry point.
5. A rotating carousel as the primary hero (D3 forbids it).
6. Giant pill / blob shapes as the default; inconsistent radius between pages.
7. A drop shadow on every card; more than one shadow "language".
8. Random decorative blobs / arbitrary gradient circles (AI-landing-page filler).
9. More than one primary CTA color; CTA color changing by page.
10. Marketplace-tiny typography; cramped card text.
11. Huge desktop whitespace that collapses badly on mobile (desktop-first thinking).
12. Hover-only behavior with no tap/keyboard equivalent.
13. Emoji as the production icon system; mixed icon families.
14. Product images cropped through packaging/label text; per-view inconsistent ratios.
15. A checkout styled like a marketing page (banners, cross-sell, promo strips).
16. Fake social proof — review counts, "X đã mua", certification badges, guarantees,
    invented return/delivery promises.
17. Visual language that changes page to page (the "Site A / Site B / Site C" failure).
18. Status conveyed by color alone.
19. A success page that reads as "delivered / complete" (celebration, "Đã giao").
20. A "Theo dõi đơn hàng" button on the success page that links to its own URL.
21. Two stacked bottom sticky bars; filter + CTA both sticky; a sticky transactional bar
    on mobile checkout.
22. Greying an out-of-stock `ProductCard` into near-invisibility.
23. Freezing any hex / spacing / radius / type-scale value as "the token" before S2.4.

## 26. Structured moodboard (A–L)

*(Per brief §41. Text moodboard — no Figma, no fabricated screenshots. For each
category: desired · avoid · relevance. "References" are inspiration categories only —
none inspected, see §30.)*

### A. Brand feeling
- **Desired:** real, caring mother-baby shop; calm and competent; quietly friendly;
  charm in small details (a whale/wave mark, soft corners, human copy).
- **Avoid:** toy site; clinical; luxury-fashion cool; AI landing page; marketplace
  clutter.
- **Relevance:** this is the perception goal the whole system serves (S2.1 §5).

### B. Color atmosphere
- **Desired:** light warm-neutral canvas; blue as the structural trust anchor; pink as a
  small warmth accent; near-zero decorative gradients; generous negative space.
- **Avoid:** pink/blue as page backgrounds; rainbow category tiles; gradient per section;
  CTA color drift.
- **Relevance:** `storefront-ui-design` ("blue as trust foundation, pink as accent, not
  dominant"); accessibility (contrast).

### C. Typography character
- **Desired:** humanist, friendly-credible, superb Vietnamese diacritics, strong price
  numerals; confident (not billboard) headings; one family (Be Vietnam Pro), many
  weights.
- **Avoid:** childish rounded display faces; clinical grotesques; marketplace-tiny body;
  a second display font for MVP.
- **Relevance:** S1 font foundation; `vietnamese-ecommerce-ui`; price legibility.

### D. Product imagery
- **Desired:** one aspect ratio site-wide (square); consistent light frame; `contain`-fit
  with padding; labels never cropped; branded neutral missing-image placeholder.
- **Avoid:** mixed ratios; edge-to-edge crops through text; broken-image glyphs; stock
  lifestyle substitutes; recoloring source photos.
- **Relevance:** `next-image-storefront`; supplier photos vary; §13.

### E. Product cards
- **Desired:** image-dominant; quiet metadata; **price is the loudest text**; calm stock
  badge (loud only for "Hết hàng"); subtle hover; visible focus ring; 2-up at 390px;
  reserved name height; no Add-to-Cart (D2).
- **Avoid:** decorated frames competing with the product; add-to-cart on the card; long
  names pushing price out of view; greyed-out OOS cards.
- **Relevance:** the reusability test (S2.1 §48); card guidance (§5.2).

### F. Product detail
- **Desired:** large calm gallery → confident name → **prominent price** → honest
  availability → sized quantity → one blue "Thêm vào giỏ"; sticky bottom bar after
  scroll; quiet specs/trust; OOS = disabled "Hết hàng" + related surfaced.
- **Avoid:** aggressive CTAs, urgency banners, caps; price buried under promos; colored
  bands around the photo.
- **Relevance:** the north-star screen (S2.1 §47); `ecommerce-ux` hierarchy.

### G. Header / search
- **Desired:** light, compact, hairline-bordered; modest logo; **prominent search**
  (full-width row on mobile); minimal nav; small pink cart badge; faint elevation only
  when stuck.
- **Avoid:** thick colored bar; tall sticky header; search hidden behind an icon; login
  entry (none in MVP).
- **Relevance:** S2.1 §3; mobile breadcrumb/context guidance.

### H. Cart / checkout
- **Desired:** confident, not exciting; clear line separation; sized quantity controls;
  "Tổng tiền hàng" strong, shipping-note plain; price/stock changes as **informational**
  (not error) treatments; calm single-page checkout; payment as bordered selectable
  rows; sticky summary (desktop) / collapsible (mobile); in-flow submit on mobile
  checkout.
- **Avoid:** error-screen styling for recoverable states; marketing in checkout;
  "Tổng thanh toán"; bottom sticky bar on mobile checkout.
- **Relevance:** S2.1 §8–§10, §26–§27; checkout trust test (§49); published one-page +
  trust-signal guidance.

### I. Trust cues
- **Desired:** small icon + short true statement; grouped; low emphasis; consistent on
  Home / PDP / Checkout / Success / Footer; factual only (contact, COD/transfer, human
  confirmation).
- **Avoid:** fake reviews/badges/guarantees; colored trust panels; "100% chính hãng"
  without owner approval; lorem policy text.
- **Relevance:** brief §30; `baby-wale-domain` (no fabricated guarantees).

### J. Mobile sticky actions
- **Desired:** compact sticky header + one bottom action bar (Product: Add-to-Cart;
  Cart: Checkout); detached by top hairline + soft overlay shadow; limited height.
- **Avoid:** two stacked bottom bars; sticky filter + sticky CTA; sticky transactional
  bar on checkout; tall sticky surfaces crushing content.
- **Relevance:** refinement A; `mobile-first-storefront`.

### K. Iconography
- **Desired:** one line-icon set, single stroke weight; icon + text for commerce
  concepts; a few restrained brand moments.
- **Avoid:** emoji icons; mixed families; decorative icons on every row; icon-only
  unfamiliar actions.
- **Relevance:** `visual-consistency` (one icon set); accessibility (names).

### L. Empty / error states
- **Desired:** a heading + a short human line + a real recovery action; distinct
  loading / empty / not-found / error / blocked treatments; optional small whale/wave
  motif on empty; calm (not alarming) for recoverable cart/checkout states; never raw
  errors.
- **Avoid:** blank screens; identical treatment for "empty" vs "error"; red-wash for
  recoverable states; broken-image glyphs; stack traces / codes.
- **Relevance:** S2.1 §17; `storefront-error-handling`.
